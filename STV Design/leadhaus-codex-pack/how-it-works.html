The Five-Step Process

How we build your pipeline from zero to predictable.

Step 01

Discovery & alignment

We start by understanding your business deeply — your product, value proposition, existing customers, deal sizes, and where your best historical wins came from. No templates. No copy-paste strategy.

In week one we run a structured intake session with your commercial leadership. We map your competitive landscape, review your existing pipeline data, and benchmark your current conversion rates. This becomes the intelligence layer for everything that follows.
Step 02

ICP mapping & list build

We define your ideal customer profile with precision — industry, headcount band, revenue range, tech stack, buying signals, and organisational trigger events that indicate readiness to purchase.

We build verified, GDPR-compliant contact lists using a combination of proprietary data sources, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and manual research. Every contact is validated before it enters a sequence. We do not buy lists.
Step 03

Messaging & positioning

We write bespoke copy for every channel — email, LinkedIn, and phone scripts. The messaging is grounded in your buyer's world, not generic templates about "synergy" and "disruption."

We run A/B testing on subject lines, openers, and value propositions from day one. Winning variants are scaled; poor performers are retired within the first two weeks. Messaging is a living system, not a one-time brief.
Step 04

Multi-channel outreach

Our team executes across email, LinkedIn, and phone in co-ordinated sequences. We handle replies, objections, and scheduling — your sales team only appears when a prospect is qualified and keen.

We run sequences of 6–9 touchpoints over 21 days. Every negative response is recorded and used to refine targeting. Every positive response is triaged within one business hour. Nothing falls through the cracks.
Step 05

Qualified handoff & reporting

Warm introductions land in your calendar. Each comes with a briefing note — company context, prospect background, conversation summary, and relevant talking points for your meeting.

You receive a live dashboard showing activity metrics (contacts reached, opens, replies), pipeline metrics (meetings booked, no-shows, conversion rates), and financial metrics (pipeline value, cost per meeting). Monthly review calls keep strategy aligned with results.

What Sets Us Apart

The principles we operate by.

Transparency

You own everything we build.

Every contact list, every message thread, every data point is yours. We document our process so you're never dependent on us. If you leave, you take the system with you — and it keeps working.

Quality First

Volume is a vanity metric.

We optimise for qualified meetings, not raw activity numbers. A 3% reply rate with 40% conversion to meeting is better than a 12% reply rate that goes nowhere. We track what matters to revenue.

Expert Teams

Senior practitioners, not juniors.

Every campaign is managed by someone who has run outbound at scale. No account managers shuffling work to interns. The person who pitches you is the person who runs your account.

Partnership

We're paid when you win.

Our pricing includes a performance component tied to meetings delivered and pipeline generated. Our incentives are aligned with yours — not with the number of emails we can blast in a month.

Integrations

Works with the stack you already have.

Every lead is automatically logged, scored, and routed into your CRM. No manual data entry. No double-handling.
